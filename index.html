Oracle and the Database Wars of 2026

October 6, 2026 By AI Conference London Editorial

AI dominance race is no longer just about the models; it has become a fierce battle fought in the data layer. As enterprises scramble to deploy production-grade generative AI, the underlying database has emerged as the critical checkpoiny and strategic prize. This August 2026, legacy titan Oracle has made a series of aggressive moves, leveraging its vast enterprise footprint to redefine the terms of AI database wars.

The narrative for the past two years has centred on vector embeddings, with a Cambrian explosion of specialised vector databases vying for developer attention. However, Oracle's major announcement this month signals a strategic shift away from this niche. The company unveiled a significant update to its Autonomous Database, integrating native support for multi-modal data types—including video, audio, and complex 3D schematics—directly within its converged architecture. This allows enterprises to perform similarity searches not just on text but on a composite of data inputs, a crucial capability for industries like manufacturing, healthcare, and media.
